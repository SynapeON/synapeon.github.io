ThingsTerminal

A simple terminal for talking to hardware from your phone: connect over USB serial or TCP, send commands and read the answers, without an account.

What it connects to

More connections and features are added step by step in updates.

Made for the workbench

Console that stays readable

Text and HEX views, timestamps, line endings and clear receive settings, so words do not arrive in pieces.

Private by design

No account. Your settings, commands and logs stay on your phone.

Free, with PRO

Free with a small banner. PRO removes the ads and adds a custom baud rate and more line-ending and receive options: monthly, yearly or once for life.

Your language

English, Azerbaijani, Turkish, Russian, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese and Chinese.
