Privacy Policy

ThingsTerminal (com.synapeon.thingsterminal) · Last updated: 22 November 2026

ThingsTerminal is made by SynapeON. This page explains what the app does with information. In short: the app has no account, we do not run a server that collects your data, and your settings, saved commands and logs stay on your phone.

The app is released step by step, so your version may have fewer features than described here. This page covers all of them. A permission below is used only by a feature that is in your version of the app.

Data summary

  • Collected by the app: nothing. We have no server and no account.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show and measure ads in the free version (see Ads below).
  • IP address: seen by the services the app contacts, such as Google AdMob for ads, like any web request. A device or server you connect to yourself also sees your IP address.
  • Not collected: your name, e-mail, contacts, location, photos, files, messages or audio.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, saved commands (macros and sequences), the list of recent and saved connections (device names, addresses and ports), the console log, session history and log files are saved only on your phone. Passwords for MQTT are not saved.
  • We cannot see them. If you uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings, they are deleted.
  • A log or backup file is created only when you ask for it. It is stored on your phone, and it leaves the phone only if you yourself share it with another app.

The devices and servers you connect to

ThingsTerminal is a tool for talking to your own hardware. It connects only to the USB adapter, Bluetooth device, address or broker that you choose. The data you send and receive goes directly between your phone and that device or server. It does not pass through us, and we cannot see it.

  • A server you connect to (for example a TCP server or an MQTT broker) has its own privacy policy.
  • TCP, UDP and plain WebSocket connections are not encrypted. MQTT and WebSocket can use TLS where you turn it on. Use untrusted networks with care.

Permissions and why the app asks for them

  • USB host: to talk to a USB serial adapter through an OTG cable. Android asks you to allow access to each device.
  • Bluetooth (scan and connect): to find and connect to Bluetooth Low Energy and Bluetooth Classic devices. Scanning is not used to find your location. On Android 11 and older the system also requires the location permission for Bluetooth scanning. The app does not read or store your location.
  • Internet and network state: for TCP, UDP, WebSocket and MQTT connections you set up, for ads and for purchases.
  • Notifications: short notices when a connection is made or lost, also while the app is in the background.
  • Vibration: feedback when you tap a quick button.

Ads

The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob. AdMob may use an advertising identifier of your device and similar data to show and measure ads, under Google's Privacy Policy. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the app first shows a consent form, and you can change your choice later in Settings. You can also reset or limit the advertising ID in the Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads). Ads are removed in PRO.

Purchases

PRO (monthly, yearly or lifetime) is bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google. We do not receive your card or payment details. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Google Play.

Children

The app is a tool for hardware and electronics work. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data leaves the phone only in the cases above (ads, purchases, the devices and servers you connect to), or when you yourself share something, such as a log file, with another app.

Open-source software

The app uses open-source libraries. Their licences are published at synapeon.com/mobile/things-terminal/licenses.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date. Questions: support.synapeon@gmail.com.
