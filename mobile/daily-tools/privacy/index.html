Privacy Policy

Daily Tools (com.synapeon.dailytools) · Last updated: 12 November 2026

Daily Tools is made by SynapeON. This page explains what the app does with information. In short: the app has no account, we do not run a server that collects your data, and your notes, lists and settings stay on your phone.

What stays on your device

  • Notes, lists, habits, income and expenses, favourites, settings and the results of tools are saved only on your phone.
  • We cannot see them. If you uninstall the app they are deleted with it.

Permissions and why the app asks for them

Every permission is asked only when a tool needs it, and what the tool reads is used on the phone and not sent to us.

  • Camera: QR and barcode scanner, document scanner, mirror, flashlight and measuring tools.
  • Microphone: sound meter, tuner, voice notes, speech to text and tone tools. Audio is processed on the phone. Speech to text uses the speech service of your device, which has its own privacy policy.
  • Location: compass, parking, speed and the Wi-Fi tools. The position is used on the phone and is not stored on our side.
  • Physical activity: the step counter.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: timers, countdowns and goal alerts.
  • Network and Wi-Fi state: to show the connection and for the IP and Wi-Fi tools.
  • Vibration, NFC and sensors: haptics, the NFC reader and tools such as the level, compass and metal detector.

Internet

  • The currency converter downloads exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API. The request contains no personal data, only the technical data every web request has (such as your IP address).
  • The time zone, moon and most other tools work without the internet.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use an advertising identifier of your device and similar data to show and measure ads, under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or limit the advertising ID in the Android settings (Settings, Google, Ads). Ads are removed in Pro.

Purchases

Pro (monthly, yearly or lifetime) is bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google. We do not receive your card or payment details. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in Google Play.

Children

The app is a general tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data leaves the phone only in the cases above (ads, purchases, exchange rates), or when you yourself share something, for example a note or a scan, with another app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date. Questions: support.synapeon@gmail.com.
