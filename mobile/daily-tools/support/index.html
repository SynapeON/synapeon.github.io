Support

Daily Tools

Write to support.synapeon@gmail.com with your question or problem. Please add your phone model and Android version and, if you can, what you did before the problem.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Google Play, tap your profile picture, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, choose Daily Tools and tap Cancel.

I bought Pro but it is not active

Open Pro in the app and use the restore option, with the same Google account that bought it. If it still does not work, write to us with the order number.

Where are the other tools?

The app is released step by step. New tools are added in updates, so your version may have fewer tools than the description of the full app. Update the app in Google Play to get new tools.

The compass is not accurate

Move the phone in a figure-eight, and keep it away from magnets, metal and electronics. The app shows a note when the sensor needs calibration.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is stored on your phone. Clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.
